iPhone FM wiring — Plan B

Alternative to Plan A, not a replacement. Swaps the STM32F072 for a Microchip ATSAMD51G18A (U1) and adds a Si4704 FM receiver (U4) for scanning and listening. J1, U3 and U2 are unchanged. Lines are nets; short stubs are parts local to one chip; “▲ 3V3 rail” means wired to U3’s 3.3 V output. Tap a pin for its datasheet source.
