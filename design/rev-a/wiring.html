IphoneFM Rev A wiring

Rev A, the active design (analog FM, option D2). Plan C is archived (design/archive/plan-c/ in the repo). The LMX2571’s internal VCO makes the carrier and U1 streams the FM deviation to it over I²S, so there is no analog modulator. The power level comes from a DIP switch on a step attenuator, and receive is the Si4705 as before. U1’s pins were checked against DS14217 Tables 26–28 and the ROM bootloader’s pins (AN2606 Table 165). Lines are nets; short stubs are parts local to one chip; “▲ 3V3” means wired to U3’s 3.3 V output. Tap a pin for its datasheet source.
