# Rev B footprints

Rev A's footprints (Phase 2), unchanged, except the front panel (Rev B Phase B3, 2026-11-06). The wheels RV1–RV3 and
their footprint are gone. SW3 is now the Diptronics DHN-06-V. U8, DS1–DS4 and SW4 are new. Each new part was looked for
in KiCad's library first; none has a KiCad footprint (KiCad has no 5 × 5 mm 0.4 mm-pitch QFN-44, no GS3011 and no SIQ
encoder), so U8, DS1–DS4 and SW4 are built from the maker's own land pattern by `tools/fp_rev_b.py`. SW3 keeps KiCad's
Omron A6H-6101 pads, which are Diptronics' layout exactly. Each was checked against its maker's drawing on a to-scale
overlay ([../../design/rev-b/footprint-overlays.png](../../design/rev-b/footprint-overlays.png)).

LCSC's own footprints (EasyEDA, read 2026-11-06) were read as a second opinion only; the makers' drawings decide:
- GS3011AW-B: matches Guangcai exactly.
- SIQ-02FVS3: matches Mitsumi except the two angled frame tabs, whose centres LCSC puts 4.0 mm from the knob centre.
  Mitsumi's "2–4" is to the tabs' inner ends: the PDF's own vectors put the centres 4.60 mm out.
- IS31FL3236A: narrower 0.20 × 0.90 mm pads, and no joins to the centre pad.
- DHN-06-V: rows 5.25 / 7.95 mm, not Diptronics' 4.90 / 7.40.

The Rev A text below is kept as it was; its SW3 and RV1–RV3 rows are replaced by the Rev B rows.

# Rev A footprints (Phase 2)

Each part's footprint source, chosen in the order step 2.1 sets: KiCad's own library, then the maker's own land pattern, then LCSC/EasyEDA. A KiCad footprint named for the exact part is preferred over a generic one. Nothing here is final until step 2.2 has compared its pads with the datasheet's land pattern; that column records the result.

**Silkscreen (step 2.4, 2026-10-23):** every footprint the board uses is a copy in `IphoneFM.pretty` whose silkscreen `tools/fp_silk_trim.py` has widened to 0.25 mm and cut back to stay 0.20 mm from copper and holes (fab-rules.md); the Footprint column below names each one's source. The trimming leaves no outline on the 0402 and 0603 parts, the test pads and D5 (all work either way round); every pin-1 mark is a round dot 0.55 mm across (the user's choice over KiCad's triangles), moved outward where needed, and D1 and Y2 get one beside pad 1 in place of their trimmed corner marks. Run fp_silk_trim.py after any of the fp_*.py builders.

**Followed up with the user at their 2.2 steps (asked 2026-10-22):** U4 and SW2; see their rows.

**Rule for checking (agreed 2026-10-22):** a KiCad *package* footprint (generic, computed to IPC-7351B nominal, our Phase 0 convention) is kept when what must match does: pin count, pitch, pin 1 and numbering, body size and any exposed pad's size; pad-shape differences from a maker's "footprint example" don't count against it. A footprint copied from one maker's drawing (like J1), or one we build, must match that maker's drawing exactly.

Sources: **KiCad (part)** is a KiCad library footprint named for this part; **KiCad (generic)** is a KiCad footprint for the package, used only if its pads match the maker's land pattern; **Maker** is drawn from the land pattern in the maker's datasheet. No part needs LCSC/EasyEDA.
