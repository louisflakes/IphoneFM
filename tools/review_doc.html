IphoneFM Rev A — design review

MagSafe FM transmitter / receiver for iPhone (USB-C audio). Prepared for an independent sanity check before the JLCPCB order. 26 Oct 2026. Every claim cites its datasheet page; the datasheets are in the package.

1. Overview

What it is. A small board that clips to the back of an iPhone and plugs into its USB-C port. The phone sees a USB audio device. In transmit it broadcasts the phone's audio on a chosen FM channel (88.1–107.9 MHz, low-power, FCC Part 15 target). In receive it tunes an FM station and streams it back to the phone.

How. The MCU does all the audio work. The radio is two single-chip parts: a TI LMX2571 synthesizer generates the FM carrier directly (the MCU streams the modulation to it as frequency steps), and a Skyworks Si4705 receives. They share one SMA antenna through an RF switch set by a slider.

Board46.45 × 71.40 mm, 2 layers, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz (JLC0216A stack-up). Ground poured on both layers, 303 vias (123 stitching).
Parts94 placed (45 types), all on the top side; 5 bare test pads underneath. Fully assembled by JLCPCB.
PowerUSB-C 5 V from the phone → AP2112K 3.3 V LDO (600 mA). ~96 mA transmitting, ~62 mA receiving, plus ≤ 15 mA LED. Inside Apple's 100 mA (before USB set-up) / 500 mA limits.
Transmit88.1–107.9 MHz, direct digital FM ±75 kHz, stereo multiplex built in the MCU. Level: LMX2571 −18…+3 dBm, then a 0–31.5 dB DIP-switch attenuator, then a 5-pole low-pass (harmonics est. ≤ −53 dBc).
ReceiveSi4705, I²S digital audio to the MCU, then to the phone as USB audio.
ReferenceOne 20 MHz XO (±20 ppm) clocks the MCU, the synthesizer and, through an MCU timer, the receiver.
RF line50 Ω grounded coplanar, 0.92 mm track, 0.20 mm gap. Bottom layer under the RF section is solid ground except two short 3V3 links.
Controls3 thumbwheels (channel digits), TX/RX slider, 6-bit power DIP switch, BOOT button, RGB LED.
Cost2 assembled boards (of 5 made) ≈ $190 before shipping (JLC quote, 26 Oct 2026).

Fabrication and assembly

  • Our own 1-up panel (KiKit): 6.5 mm rails all round, because parts overhang every edge (wheels, SMA barrel, USB-C, switches); 5 mouse-bite tabs; 3 fiducials.
  • DRC: 0 errors. One rule exception: J1's two unplated locating holes sit 0.22 mm from its pads (maker's land pattern; JLC minimum 0.20 mm).
  • Placement file corrected per part against JLC's own footprints (rotation and origin), and checked in JLC's preview. JLC engineer placement confirmation is on.
  • Gerbers read back with an independent viewer and match the board.

Firmware plan (not written yet)

  1. Load: STM32 ROM USB bootloader (hold BOOT while plugging in); SWD pads as backup.
  2. USB: Audio Class 2, 48 kHz stereo, asynchronous with feedback (Apple's recommendation).
  3. TX: MCU builds the FM stereo multiplex (75 µs pre-emphasis, 19 kHz pilot, 38 kHz L−R), corrects the PLL response, interpolates to ~912 kSa/s and streams it to the LMX2571 over SAI in FSK I²S mode (TI SNAA309). LMX2571 set up over bit-banged MICROWIRE; lock watched on MUXout.
  4. RX: Si4705 over I²C; its I²S audio into the second SAI block, back to the phone.
  5. UI: wheels on the ADC → channel; slider read for mode (it also switches the RF path directly); LED shows state.

2. Signal chains

Each chain is drawn on the actual schematic; the numbered badges follow the signal. Page references are to the datasheet in the package.

Power and USB

1 J1 USB-C→2 R1/R2 Rd + D3/D4 ESD→3 U6 ESD (D+, D−, VBUS)→4 U3 LDO → 3V3
  • 5.1 kΩ Rd on each CC line makes the phone switch on 5 V; no USB-PD needed. USB Type-C R2.5 Table 4-28 p246
  • USBLC6 at the connector on D+, D− and VBUS; TPD1E05U06 on each CC. USBLC6-2 p1–2
  • AP2112K: 600 mA, stable with ≥ 1 µF; load ≤ ~110 mA. AP2112 p1–2
  • Ferrites split the ADC supply (VDDA, FB2) and the receiver supply (FB3) from digital 3V3. DS14217 Fig. 23 p127; Si4705 p16

Reference clock

1 Y2 20 MHz XO→U2 OSCin and 2 R4 33 Ω → U1 PH0
  • CMOS straight into OSCin, the input TI recommends for best spurs. LMX2571 Table 37 p38, p11
  • U1 runs in HSE bypass from the same clock. DS14217 p184–185
  • U1 timer makes 32,787 Hz for the Si4705's RCLK (31.1–34.4 kHz allowed). AN332 p37

MCU

1 U1 STM32U535→2 R6–R8 33 Ω FSK I²S·3 FB2 VDDA
  • Bypassing per ST's power scheme; 4.7 µF on VCAP. DS14217 p127
  • Pins checked against the alternate-function and bootloader tables. DS14217 Tables 26–28; AN2606 Table 165

Transmit

1 U2 LMX2571→2 loop filter·3 C26 DC block→4 U7 attenuator←5 SW3→6 FL1 low-pass→7 U5 switch→8 D5 ESD→9 J2
  • LMX2571 per TI's typical schematic: 100 nF on every supply pin, 2.2 µF on VregVCO, VcpExt powered. LMX2571 Fig. 62 p44; p53–54
  • Loop filter 100 pF / 1.1 kΩ / 1.5 nF → 300–405 kHz loop, ~61° margin; flat to 15 kHz, +0.6 dB at 53 kHz (MCU pre-corrects). Charge-pump current is a firmware setting. LMX2571 p5–6, p35
  • Divider ÷56 / ÷48 and two reference settings give ±100 kHz deviation headroom on all 100 US channels. LMX2571 Eq. 4 p35; SNAA309 §6.2
  • PE43711 in direct parallel mode from the DIP switch; pull-downs make an open switch read 0. PE43711 p8, Table 4 p6
  • FL1: 5-pole 50 Ω low-pass, 23–34 dB at the 2nd harmonic, ≥ 43 dB from 264 MHz. 47 CFR §15.239 / §15.209
  • PE42421: ~85 dB isolation keeps the TX carrier off the receiver; the slider drives its CTRL pin directly. PE42421 p3–4

Receive

J2 → U5 RF1→1 C34 1 nF→2 L1 150 nH match→3 FB1 + C35 filter→4 U4 Si4705→5 R22–R24 → U1 SAI
  • Front end, emissions filter and I²S series parts as Skyworks specifies. Si4705 p16, Table 12 p18; AN383 p7–9, p37
  • U2 is powered down (CE) while receiving, so its carrier can't desensitise U4. LMX2571 p8

Controls

1 RV1–RV3 → ADC·2 SW1 → RF_MODE·3 SW2 BOOT0·4 D1 LED
  • Wheels are 10 kΩ dividers 3V3–GND, read by the ADC; firmware splits each into 10 digits.
  • Slider: 3V3 = TX, GND = RX, 100 kΩ holds RX between contacts; it drives U5 and is read by U1. PE42421 p4
  • BOOT0 pulled low; pressed at plug-in → ROM USB bootloader. AN2606 p30
  • LED anode on VBUS (blue/green need up to 3.4 V), sunk by U1's 5 V-tolerant open-drain pins. XL-1615 p3–4; DS14217 p132

3. What I'd most like checked

  1. Direct FM with the LMX2571. No published schematic uses it this way; is the loop filter (C24, R5, C25) and the FSK I²S approach sound?
  2. RF layout on 2 layers: the 50 Ω line, ground stitching and return paths under U2, U5 and U4, and TX-to-RX isolation.
  3. FL1 values (33 pF, 100 nH, 56 pF, 100 nH, 33 pF) against a square-wave source, and whether its harmonic margin is enough for Part 15.
  4. USB-C sink: Rd only, no PD controller; current budget against Apple's limits.
  5. Clocking: one 20 MHz XO for MCU and synthesizer; 32,787 Hz from a timer for the Si4705.
  6. Manufacturing: the panel and rails, the J1 hole-clearance exception, the JLC placement corrections.
  7. Anything that would stop it working on first power-up.
